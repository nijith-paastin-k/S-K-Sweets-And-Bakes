# Analytics & Financial Reporting Definitions — S K Sweets & Bakes

## 1. Metrics & Formula Definitions

1. **Gross Sales**:
   $$\text{Gross Sales} = \sum (\text{Line Item Quantities} \times \text{Unit Prices})$$
2. **Discounts**:
   $$\text{Discounts} = \sum \text{Coupons Applied} + \sum \text{Loyalty Redemptions} + \sum \text{Authorized Counter Discounts}$$
3. **Net Sales**:
   $$\text{Net Sales} = \text{Gross Sales} - \text{Discounts} - \text{Refunds}$$
4. **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**:
   $$\text{COGS} = \sum (\text{Units Sold} \times \text{Unit Cost})$$
5. **Gross Profit**:
   $$\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Net Sales} - \text{COGS}$$
6. **Gross Margin %**:
   $$\text{Gross Margin} = \left (\frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Net Sales}}\right) \times 100$$
7. **Operating Expenses**:
   $$\text{Operating Expenses} = \sum (\text{Rent} + \text{Wages} + \text{Electricity} + \text{Packaging} + \text{Delivery} + \text{Repairs} + \text{Misc})$$
8. **Estimated Operating Profit**:
   $$\text{Operating Profit} = \text{Gross Profit} - \text{Operating Expenses}$$
9. **Average Order Value (AOV)**:
   $$\text{AOV} = \frac{\text{Net Sales}}{\text{Total Orders}}$$

## 2. Product Velocity Classification

- **Best-Selling Products**: Top 20% by sales volume and revenue in the designated reporting period.
- **Average-Performing Products**: Middle 60% with consistent movement.
- **Slow-Moving Products**: Bottom 20% where item has been active and in-stock for $\ge 14$ days but
  generated low or zero turnover.
- **Out of Stock**: Filtered separately so that unavailable items are not mistakenly labelled as
  slow-moving.
